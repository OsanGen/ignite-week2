RECORDS BEFORE TOTALS

One record should explain one cost.

A total is only useful when another person can follow it back to the inputs. A cost record should identify the work, the source, the units and the calculation.

Terms we will use

Source reference: where the input came from. In this packet, it points to a training table or an explicitly synthetic invoice excerpt.

Record identity: the label used to distinguish one record from another. A copied record and a separate billable event are not the same thing.

Payer: the party responsible for the charge. Do not assume it is DMS merely because DMS is the project client.

Allocation: a stated method for assigning a share of a common cost. The exercise uses $120.00 divided across 10 equal-weight same-month course runs. That is not an approved DMS allocation policy.

Mapping: the relationship between a source field and the field we propose to use. Without a real DMS source schema, this remains a proposed mapping.

THE PROPOSED REVIEW WORKFLOW

Source → map → validate → reconcile → report.

  1. Source

    Preserve the original input and identify the course, run and record.

  2. Map

    Match the source value to the right field. Keep its unit and meaning attached.

  3. Validate

    Check whether required values are present and usable. Keep missing, invalid and duplicate cases visible.

  4. Reconcile

    Compare amounts that cover the same service, quantity, period and scope. Record differences without hiding them.

  5. Report

    Show the supported calculation, its source references and any unresolved questions.

Boundary

This describes the behavior we are preparing to review. It is not proof that the current IGNITE build already performs every step.

ONE FORMULA, EXPLICIT UNITS

Make units agree before adding costs.

Labor

Hours × hourly cost rate = labor cost.

Example: 2.0 hours × $60.00 per hour = $120.00.

Provider usage

Usage quantity ÷ billing quantity × rate = usage cost.

Example: 300,000 tokens ÷ 1,000,000 tokens × $2.00 = $0.60.

Shared hosting

Monthly pool ÷ eligible same-month runs = allocated cost.

Example: $120.00 ÷ 10 = $12.00.

Exercise convention

Use USD. Round each cost row to two decimal places, half up, then add the rounded rows. This is the fixed training convention, not an approved production policy.

PROTECT THE MEANING OF THE DATA

Missing is not zero. Repeated is not new.

Missing

A blank labor-hours field does not establish zero hours. Flag the missing value instead of presenting a complete total.

Invalid

A zero billing divisor, negative labor hours or incompatible units cannot silently become a valid cost.

Duplicate

Replaying the same L01 record must not silently add another $120.00.

Distinct attempt

A separate billable video attempt may add cost even when it produces no additional delivered video.

Open rule

Reject, hold, ignore and update are different responses. Where the real policy is undecided, propose the response and record the decision still needed.

For this preparation assignment, application results remain Not run.

Keep two kinds of review separate

Financial question: Is the amount, unit, allocation or scope supported by the supplied exercise?

Technical question: What should the application do with this input, and how would we observe that behavior on a named build?

A proposed error message is not an observed error message. A reviewed test case is not a passed test. An unresolved question is a valid preparation result when the missing decision is specific.

What we know

What we do not know from the difference alone

whether the cause is another attempt, a period mismatch, a duplicate charge or an error.

Review response

Keep the difference open. Request the missing evidence. Do not change the original baseline just to make the numbers agree.

When the extra attempt is explicitly supplied

The separate Week 1 R1 scenario states that one additional minute was billed but discarded. When that fact is supplied as part of the training evidence:

  • Recorded video usage becomes 7 billable minutes and $17.50.
  • Total provider usage becomes $20.26.
  • Direct production cost becomes $567.26.
  • Final deliverables stay at 15, including 3 delivered videos.

The extra minute explains the invoice difference in that separate scenario. The invoice alone did not prove the cause.

Keep SYN-RUN-001 at $564.76. Use the separate SYN-RUN-001-R1 scenario for $567.26. Do not add the two complete scenarios together as if they were independent course totals.

Labor and hosting stay fixed only to isolate this exercise. A real retry could require additional labor or other resources.

Do not count the invoice twice: a bill used to check an already recorded usage cost is not automatically another cost event. Adding the invoice total on top of the same underlying usage would double-count that cost.
